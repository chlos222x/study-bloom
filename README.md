# StudyBloom ✿
A calm revision hub for GCSE, A Level and BTEC students — with topics, flashcards, quizzes and progress tracking.

## How to use
Just open `index.html` in any modern browser. Everything works locally on your device.

**Features:**
- Qualification → Subject → Exam Board → Topics flow
- Topic pages with notes, flashcards & quizzes
- To-do list saved on your device
- Progress counters
- Theme switcher
- Local profile demo

> ⚠️ This is a front-end demo — all data is stored locally in your browser, not on a server.
